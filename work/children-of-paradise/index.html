The challenge

Context

For a nonprofit with decades of community work behind it, the website is the front door for donors, families, and partners. Children of Paradise runs programs in anti-violence, mental health, Abilities Over Disabilities, and scholarships, and the site needed to make that work clear and make giving easy.

It also needed to be something the team could keep current themselves, without waiting on a developer for every update.

What I built

Approach
  • A complete website rebuild of the organization's public site.
  • A redesigned donor experience, with dedicated donation pages built around making it simple to give.
  • A self-editable workflow so staff manage their own content day to day.

The result

Outcome

The rebuild and donor experience redesign resulted in a 33% increase in total donations.

The same approach applies to any small organization whose website has to do real work: make the next step obvious, remove friction from the moment someone decides to act, and give the team control of their own content.
