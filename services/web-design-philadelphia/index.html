What a Woolph website includes

Scope

Custom design & build

Designed and coded for your business and your customers — hand-built HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, or Shopify and Liquid when you are selling products.

Mobile-first, built for speed

Pages that load fast and work on a phone first, because that is where most local customers will find you.

Booking, forms & follow-up

Questions, answered

FAQ
How much does a website cost?

It depends on scope: a single landing page is a different project from a full site with booking and automation. Services are available individually or as a bundled monthly retainer, and pricing is discussed after a brief conversation about your goals.

Do you work with businesses outside Philadelphia?

Yes. I am based in Philadelphia and work with clients across the Philadelphia region, including South Jersey, and remotely beyond it.

Can I update the site myself?

If you want to. Sites like Children of Paradise and Ms. Darkchild were built with self-serve workflows so staff or the owner manage content without a developer. If you would rather not touch it, I handle changes on a maintenance plan.

Will the site rank on Google?

No one can honestly promise rankings. What I build in is the foundation: fast pages, clean structure, structured data, and local-search basics. I can pair that with Google Business Profile setup and a review-generation campaign to help you compete for local searches.

Who will I be working with?

Aaron Williams, directly. The person who plans the strategy also writes the code and runs the automations, so there are no handoffs to slow things down.

Have a site that isn't pulling its weight?

Tell me what you are working on and where it is stuck. Typical turnaround for an initial response is under 48 hours.
